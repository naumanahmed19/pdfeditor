Everything, in one editor

A full PDF workbench — not just a viewer

Read, edit, sign, fill, convert and organize. The tools that usually cost a subscription, running entirely on your device.

True in-place text editing

Click a line and edit it directly. Inkden rewrites the original text object in its own font, size and color — no whiteout patch, nothing left behind.

Real redaction

Black out sensitive text, add whiteout, and flatten on save so removed content can't be copied back out. Built for confidential work.

Sign & annotate

Draw, type or upload a signature and reuse it. Highlight, ink, shapes, comments and image stamps — baked cleanly into the file.

Fill & design forms

Fill existing AcroForm fields, or design your own — text fields, checkboxes, radios and dropdowns become real form fields on save.

OCR for scans

Make scanned or image PDFs searchable — recognition runs locally and adds an invisible text layer so search, copy and editing just work.

Merge, split & organize

Combine PDFs and images, split or extract page ranges, and drag pages to reorder, rotate, duplicate or delete. Add watermarks and page numbers.

Local AI assistant

Summarize, explain, rewrite or translate — powered by a model that runs on your own machine (Ollama, LM Studio, or any local endpoint).

Tabs & split view

Open many documents at once and work in up to four panes side by side — compare, copy between, and edit each pane independently.

Fast reader & search

Smooth continuous scrolling, word-level full-document search, outlines, thumbnails, links and password-protected PDFs.

Privacy by architecture

Your documents never leave your device

Most PDF tools upload your file to a server. Inkden doesn't — there's no server to upload to. Every operation happens locally, in your browser or the desktop app.

Nothing is uploaded

Files are read, edited and saved on your machine. No cloud storage, no telemetry on your content, nothing to leak or subpoena.

No account, no tracking

Start editing instantly. There's no sign-up, no email, and no profile — because handling your documents shouldn't require handing over your identity.

Compliance-friendly

Because content stays on the device, Inkden fits workflows where cloud PDF tools are off-limits — legal privilege, financial data, patient records.
