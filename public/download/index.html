Edit text

Redact

Sign & annotate

Draw, type or upload a signature and reuse it. Highlights, ink, shapes and stamps — baked cleanly into the file.

Fill & design forms

Fill AcroForm fields, or design your own — text fields, checkboxes, radios and dropdowns become real fields on save.

OCR for scans

Recognition runs locally and adds an invisible text layer, so search, copy and editing just work on scanned PDFs.

Split view

Open up to four panes side by side, keep references visible, and edit one PDF while checking another.

Fast search
